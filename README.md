## Abstract

Structured Information Field Dynamics (SIFD) proposes that **awareness is the fundamental physical substrate of reality**.

Awareness is not an emergent property of matter, a phenomenon occurring inside spacetime, or an observer-dependent interpretation of an otherwise non-experiential universe.

Awareness is the primitive physical substrate itself.

Everything conventionally described as physical reality is therefore treated as structured activity within awareness.

SIFD proposes a causal hierarchy beginning with awareness and progressing through distinction, information, relation, ordered transformation, memory, space, imagination, object representation, localization, relational geometry, stable modes, and effective physical descriptions.

The central claim is:

```math
\boxed{
\text{Reality}
=
\text{structured dynamical activity of awareness}
}
```

The purpose of SIFD is not to append consciousness to existing physics, nor to force existing physical theories to emerge exactly as presently formulated.

Instead, SIFD attempts to identify the deeper structure from which the phenomena described by current physical theories arise.

General relativity, quantum field theory, quantum mechanics, the Standard Model, classical mechanics, thermodynamics, and other successful physical descriptions are therefore treated as potentially valid **effective projections** of a deeper informational reality.

Their empirical predictions may remain substantially correct while their conventional ontological interpretations are incomplete.

SIFD consequently does not require the fundamental ontology of reality to consist of particles, quantum fields, gauge groups, or spacetime geometry.

Those may be stable effective structures produced by a more primitive informational substrate.

The ultimate objective is a closed mathematical dynamics:

```
\boxed{
\Omega^*
=
\mathcal F[\Omega^*]
}
```

where $\Omega$ is the complete structured state of awareness and $\mathcal F$ is the fundamental transformation governing its evolution.

All effective physical quantities should ultimately be derivable from stable solutions of this system.
